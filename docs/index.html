About me

I’m a senior embedded systems engineer at B-Trac Solutions. I work across hardware and firmware, from bringing up prototypes to integrating equipment and testing devices in the field.

My work includes sensor interfaces and C/C++ firmware on ESP32 and FreeRTOS. I use Modbus RTU and MQTT to connect equipment to monitoring systems, and I review firmware and mentor engineers.
